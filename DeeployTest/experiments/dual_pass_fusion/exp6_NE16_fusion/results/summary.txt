WHAT FUSION BUYS ON NE16 (B -> C, 218,593 cycles = 29 %):
  im2col built once instead of twice                                   -142,229
  one NE16 job of Cout=16 instead of two of Cout=8  (209,644 -> 136,618)  -73,026   (the fused job costs +30 %, not 2x)
  preparation nodes (encode once, 2 small nodes)                           -467
  output transpose: unchanged (one 2x tensor == two 1x tensors)              +0
  -> the NE16 job retires TP_OUT = 32 output channels per pass; 8 or 16 of them in use costs nearly the same.

WHAT THE SDK ROUTE BUYS ON NE16 (A' -> B, 14.4x): one 1x1 job over Cin*K = 4 folded channels instead of 4 streamin
  dispatches with int32 accumulator round-trips through L1, requant on NE16 instead of a cluster RequantShift,
  padding + the signed-input +128 offset done by the cores while building the column.

WHERE THE FUSED NE16 RUN SPENDS ITS TIME (C):  output transpose 47 %, im2col 26 %, NE16 job 25 %, prep 2 %.
  The two cluster-side data-movement nodes (transpose + im2col) are 74 % -- the accelerator itself is a quarter.

TILING: NE16 conv 4 tiles (both tasks), im2col 1 tile, output transpose 2 (B) / 4 (C) tiles; C_cl: conv 2, transpose 4.
CODE SIZE (GAP9 GCC, llvm-nm on libdeeploygap9.a): Im2ColU8 308 B, NE16DualPerturbEncode 222 B, NE16DualBias 618 B,
  NE16WeightEncode 346 B, NE16SignedInputBias 78 B; (DualConv 1,200 B, FlatConv 454 B with GCC -- vs 1,828 / 896 B with
  Siracusa's clang).
COUNTER CAVEAT (GAP9): the profiling wrappers run on the cluster MASTER core (core 8), which dispatches the 8 workers
  and waits -- its "cycles" are the node's wall time (what we need), but its instruction/IPC/stall columns describe an
  idle core and are NOT comparable to the Siracusa core-0 figures. Cluster printf is invisible on GAP9 GVSoC; the
  records are replayed by the fabric controller after the run ("[perf] records=N" marker in the logs).
NE16 arithmetic: norm/quant = (acc*scale + bias) >> shift, truncating, saturate [-128,127] (gvsoc gap/ne16/src) --
  identical to pulp-nn's requant with the +div/2 carried in `add`; hence 0 errors without any tolerance.
